YOUR PHOTO: drop your picture in this folder.

1. Save a picture here, for example "me.jpg". PNG, JPG and SVG all work.
2. Open lib/data.ts and set this line on the SITE object:
       avatar: "/images/me.jpg",
3. Save. The About section shows your photo next to the text.

Leave avatar: "" to show no photo (the About text then fills the row).

Tip: a square crop around 600x600 works great, under about 300 KB.
